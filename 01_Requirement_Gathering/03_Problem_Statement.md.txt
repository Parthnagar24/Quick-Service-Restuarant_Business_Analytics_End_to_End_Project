Domino's India operates in a highly competitive QSR market where maintaining growth while ensuring food-safety compliance and controlling operational costs is a major challenge. Recent hygiene and food-safety issues at certain outlets highlight the need for stronger visibility into branch-level operational performance and compliance. At the same time, rising operating costs and competitive pressure can affect profitability and sustainable growth.

The problem is that management needs a data-driven way to identify branches with recurring operational or compliance risks, understand sales and cost patterns, and determine which areas require further investigation or corrective action.

This project aims to develop an analytics-oriented database and SQL analysis framework that integrates relevant sales, branch, operational, cost and quality-related data to help management identify risks, monitor business performance and prioritize data-driven actions.
