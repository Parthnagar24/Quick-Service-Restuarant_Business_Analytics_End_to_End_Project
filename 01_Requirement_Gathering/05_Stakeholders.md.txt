Stakeholders

The project is designed to support multiple stakeholders involved in QSR operations and decision-making. Senior management requires a high-level view of business performance and major operational risks. Operations managers require branch-level performance and demand patterns to identify areas requiring attention. Food safety and quality teams require visibility into inspection results, recurring violations and high-risk branches. Store managers require branch-specific sales, order and product performance. Marketing and product teams require information about customer demand and menu performance, while finance requires revenue, cost and profitability-related information where available.

The Data Analyst acts as the bridge between these stakeholders and the underlying data by ensuring that the data is cleaned, structured, analyzed and translated into actionable insights.
