A. Food Safety & Quality
------------------------
Which branches have the highest number of recorded food-safety or hygiene issues?
Which types of violations occur most frequently?
Which branches have repeated violations over time?
Are previously flagged branches showing improvement after corrective action?
Which branches should be prioritized for further investigation based on available quality data?

B. Sales & Revenue
--------------------
What is the total revenue and total order volume over time?
Which branches generate the highest and lowest revenue?
What are the monthly/weekly sales trends?
Which periods experience the highest order demand?
What is the average order value, and how does it differ across branches or periods?

C. Product Performance
------------------------
Which products generate the most revenue?
Which products have the highest order volume?
Which products consistently underperform?
Which categories contribute the most to revenue?
Are there products with high sales volume but relatively low revenue contribution?

D. Customer & Ordering Behavior
--------------------------------
What does the typical customer order look like?
What ordering patterns can be observed by time, location or order channel?
Do customers tend to place larger orders during particular periods?
If customer identifiers are available, what proportion of customers are repeat customers?
Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?

E. Cost & Efficiency
-----------------------
Which branches have the highest operating or delivery-related costs?
How do discounts affect revenue and order value?
Where is wastage occurring, if wastage data is available?
Which products or branches have the greatest gap between revenue and available cost measures?
Are there operational areas where increased sales are not translating into better economics?

F. Cross-functional Analysis
---------------------------------
Do branches with repeated quality issues show unusual commercial performance?
Are high-sales branches also operationally efficient?
Are there branches with strong sales but significant operational/quality risks?
Are there branches with weak sales but relatively good operational indicators?
Which branches should management investigate first based on a combination of commercial performance and operational risk?
